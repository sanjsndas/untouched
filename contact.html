About Untouched Earth

We are a community of gardeners, ecologists, and storytellers who believe that the garden is a place of deep connection — to the land, to each other, and to the generations that will tend it after us.

Our roots

Untouched Earth began as a small notebook of observations from a kitchen garden in the Pacific Northwest. Over time, it grew into a living archive of traditional horticultural knowledge, ecological design, and the quiet wisdom of working with the seasons.

We publish essays, field guides, and practical how-tos — always grounded in place and written with a reverence for the natural world. Our contributors are growers, botanists, and artists who share a common ethic: to tend with care and to leave the soil richer than we found it.

What we stand for

We champion open-pollinated seeds, soil food webs, perennial systems, and the preservation of heirloom varieties. We believe that gardening is a political act — one that can restore biodiversity, nourish communities, and re-enchant our relationship with the earth.

— the editors, 2026

🌱 Stewardship

We practice regenerative methods that build soil, support pollinators, and create resilient ecosystems.

📚 Heritage

We preserve and share traditional knowledge — from seed saving to natural building — that has sustained cultures for millennia.

🕊️ Accessibility

We believe that the art of gardening should be available to everyone, regardless of background or means.
