Privacy Policy

Your privacy is as important to us as the health of the soil. This policy explains how we handle your data when you visit Untouched Earth.

1. Information we collect

We collect minimal information to improve your experience and to understand how our content is read. This includes:

  • Usage data: pages visited, time spent, referring URLs (via anonymized analytics).
  • Cookies: we use a small number of essential cookies for site functionality and preference storage. For more details, see our Cookies Policy.
  • Contact forms: if you reach out via our contact page, we store your name and email address only for the purpose of responding to your inquiry.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties.

2. How we use your data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Improve our content and website performance.
  • Respond to your messages and comments.
  • Send occasional newsletters (only if you have explicitly opted in).

Your data is safe with us. We never share your personal information with advertisers or external platforms, and we store all data securely in compliance with GDPR and CCPA standards.

3. Third-party services

We use a limited number of third-party services to operate our website. These include:

  • Webflow / hosting provider — for secure hosting and content delivery.
  • Analytics (privacy-first) — we use a self-hosted analytics tool that does not track you across the web or use persistent identifiers.

All third-party processors are bound by strict data protection agreements and are located within the EU/EEA or are Privacy Shield certified.

4. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where we rely on consent).
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us directly.

5. Data retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected. For contact form inquiries, we keep records for up to 12 months. Analytics data is aggregated and anonymized after 26 months.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or legal obligations. The latest version will always be posted on this page, with the date of the last revision noted below.
