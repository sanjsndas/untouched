Cookies Policy

We use cookies sparingly and with respect for your privacy. This policy explains what we use, why, and how you can manage them.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences, understand how you interact with our content, and keep the site running smoothly. We believe in minimal, transparent cookie use.

How we use cookies

We classify cookies into three categories, each with a specific purpose. We never use tracking cookies for advertising or cross-site profiling.

TypePurposeDurationExample
Essential Necessary for core functionality: session management, security, and accessibility. Session / up to 1 year session_id, csrf_token
Functional Remember user preferences, such as language or theme selection. Up to 12 months lang_pref, theme_mode
Analytics Anonymized, privacy-first analytics to understand page usage and improve content. Up to 26 months (aggregated) visit_id (hashed)

Your choice matters. We do not use third-party marketing or ad-targeting cookies. You can manage your cookie preferences at any time through your browser settings. For more details, see our Privacy Policy.

Managing cookies

Most browsers allow you to control cookie settings. You can block or delete cookies at any time. However, disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of our site. Below are links to manage cookies in popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome – Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox – Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari – Preferences > Privacy > Cookies and website data
  • Edge – Settings > Cookies and site permissions

If you prefer, you can also opt out of analytics cookies by adjusting your browser's Do Not Track settings or using privacy extensions.

Third-party services

We use a limited set of third-party services that may place their own cookies:

  • Webflow (hosting) – may set essential session cookies for site performance.
  • Self-hosted analytics – we use a privacy-first analytics tool that does not use persistent cross-site identifiers.

All third-party processors are GDPR-compliant and do not share data with additional parties.

Updates to this policy

We review our cookie usage regularly. Any changes to this policy will be posted here with a revised date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
